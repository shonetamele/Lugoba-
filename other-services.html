Services & Ministries

Opportunities to grow, serve and impact our community for Christ.

Weekly Gatherings

Sunday Main Service

9:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Spirit-filled worship, the preaching of the Word, prayer and Holy Communion (first Sunday of the month). Everyone is welcome.

Mid-week Prayer

Wednesday 5:30 PM

A focused time of intercession for the church, the nation and personal needs. Come expectant.

Youth & Children

Saturday 3:00 PM

Age-appropriate teaching, games, worship and mentorship that helps young people walk with Jesus.

Ministries

Women’s Fellowship

Monthly gatherings for prayer, teaching and practical support. First Saturday of every month. Women are encouraged, equipped and released into their calling.

Worship & Music

Our worship team leads the congregation into the presence of God through song, instruments and creative expression.

Community Outreach

Practical acts of love — visiting the sick, supporting vulnerable families, and sharing the Gospel in the neighbourhoods around Lugoba.

Discipleship Classes

Foundational teaching for new believers and deeper study for those who desire to grow in the Word and in holiness.

Marriage & Family

Counselling, seminars and continuous support for couples and parents who want to build godly homes.

Media & Online

Live streaming, recorded messages and digital content so the Word of God can reach beyond the physical walls of the church.

Want to serve?

There is a place for every gift. Create an account or speak to any of the pastors after service.
