Who We Are

A Christ-centred family committed to holiness, faithful relationships and transforming lives across Uganda and beyond.

Our Vision

Our vision is to promote a spiritually vibrant community, committed to holiness and Christ-like living, while transforming lives and impacting the world around us. (1 Peter 1:16)

Core Values

  • Holiness
  • Faithful Relationships
  • Community Impact
  • Prayer & Worship
  • Integrity & Love

Mission

Our mission is to nurture spiritual growth, foster faithful relationships, and positively impact the community around us. We are committed to pursuing holiness and living a Christ-like life, as guided by 1 Peter 1:16.

Ministries & Departments

There is a place for every gift. Grow, serve and impact our community for Christ.

Children’s Ministry

Nurturing children in the Word of God through teaching, worship and age-appropriate fellowship.

Youth Ministry

Equipping young people to walk with Jesus, lead with integrity and impact their generation.

Women of Purpose / Girl Power

Empowering women and girls through prayer, teaching and purposeful community support.

Men’s Ministry

Building godly men who lead their homes and serve the church with strength and humility.

Choir / Praise Team

Leading the congregation into God’s presence through song, instruments and heartfelt worship.

Intercessors

Standing in the gap in prayer for the church, families, the nation and the nations.

Media Team

Capturing and sharing the Word through sound, visuals, live streaming and digital content.

Marrieds Ministry

Strengthening marriages and families through teaching, counselling and fellowship.
