Who We Are

A Christ-centred family committed to holiness, faithful relationships and transforming lives across Uganda and beyond.

Our Vision

To promote a spiritually vibrant community committed to holiness and Christ-like living, while transforming lives and impacting the world around us. (1 Peter 1:16)

Core Values

  • Holiness
  • Faithful Relationships
  • Community Impact
  • Prayer & Worship
  • Integrity & Love

Mission

To nurture spiritual growth, foster faithful relationships and positively impact the community. We pursue holiness and a Christ-like life as guided by Scripture.

Worship With Us

Join us every week for vibrant worship, the Word of God and warm fellowship. Everyone is welcome.

  • Sunday Main Service 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Mid-week Prayer Wednesday 5:30 PM
  • Youth & Children Saturday 3:00 PM
  • Women’s Fellowship 1st Saturday monthly
