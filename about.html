About Our Church

A house of prayer, holiness and community transformation since 2021.

Our Story

Lugoba Miracle Center Church was established in 2021 under the leadership of Senior Pastor Teopisther Najjuma. From a humble beginning in Lugoba along Bombo Road, the church has grown into a vibrant community of believers who are passionate about the Word of God, holiness and practical love.

We exist to connect people to Christ, nurture spiritual maturity and extend the Kingdom of God into families, workplaces and the wider East African region — Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania and beyond.

What We Believe

We believe in the authority of Scripture, the Lordship of Jesus Christ, the power of the Holy Spirit, and the call to live a life of holiness as stated in 1 Peter 1:16 — “Be holy, because I am holy.”

Our gatherings are characterised by heartfelt worship, sound teaching, fervent prayer and genuine fellowship. We place special emphasis on family, youth, women’s ministry and community outreach.

Our Foundation

Built on the solid rock of Jesus Christ. Every ministry, decision and programme is measured against the Word of God and the pursuit of holiness.

Our Community

We are a family. From children to elders, every member is valued, discipled and equipped to serve according to their God-given gifts.

Our Reach

While rooted in Lugoba, Kampala, our vision extends across East Africa and to the nations — sharing the good news of Jesus Christ wherever He opens doors.
