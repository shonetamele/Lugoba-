About Us

A house of prayer, holiness and transformed lives — from a home altar to a growing church in Lugoba.

Vision & Mission

Vision Statement

Our vision is to promote a spiritually vibrant community, committed to holiness and Christ-like living, while transforming lives and impacting the world around us. (1 Peter 1:16)

Mission Statement

Our mission is to nurture spiritual growth, foster faithful relationships, and positively impact the community around us. We are committed to pursuing holiness and living a Christ-like life, as guided by 1 Peter 1:16.

Core Values

  • Holiness
  • Faithful relationships
  • Community impact
  • Prayer and worship
  • Integrity and love

Our History

Lugoba Miracle Center Church began during the COVID-19 period, when churches across the nation were closed. In that season of lockdown, one family gathered together and raised an altar of prayer and worship in their home.

Before long, a few neighbours joined them. What started as a small home fellowship grew in faith, unity and numbers as God met them in prayer.

After COVID-19 restrictions eased, more people continued to come. The congregation outgrew the early meeting place and later shifted from a garage setting into a standard building — the house of worship we gather in today along Bombo Road in Lugoba, Kampala.

Established in the fire of prayer · Growing by the grace of God

What We Believe

We believe that Miracle Center Lugoba has changed many lives of people — spiritually, physically, socially and financially. Through the Word of God, prayer and genuine fellowship, men, women and families continue to encounter Jesus Christ and walk in holiness and hope.

“Be holy, because I am holy.” — 1 Peter 1:16
