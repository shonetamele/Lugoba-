Privacy & Policy

How we handle your personal information.

Introduction

Lugoba Miracle Center Church & Church Foundation Limited (“we”, “the Church”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect when you use our website and related services, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide when creating a fellowship account (name, email, optional phone).
  • Messages and prayer requests you send via the website or WhatsApp links.
  • Technical data such as browser type and approximate location (from analytics, if enabled).
  • Photos uploaded by authorised administrators for the public gallery.

How We Use Information

  • To manage membership and communicate church announcements.
  • To respond to enquiries and prayer requests.
  • To improve the website and the services we offer.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Account Approval

New accounts are reviewed by church administrators before full access is granted. This helps maintain a safe and orderly fellowship environment.

Storage & Security

Account data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud). Gallery images may be stored on Cloudinary. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at mcclfoundation@gmail.com or via WhatsApp on +256 704 476 063.
